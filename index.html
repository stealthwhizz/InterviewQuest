      📖 How to Play

      🎯 Objective

      Progress through three boss levels by answering interview questions. Defeat each boss to unlock the next challenge!

      ❤️ Lives System

      • Start with 3 lives
      • Lose a life when you score below 4
      • Restore a life with scores of 9-10 (max 3 lives)
      • Game over when lives reach 0

      ⭐ XP & Progression

      • Earn XP based on answer quality (1-10 score)
      • Unlock Senior Engineer at 500 XP
      • Unlock FAANG Boss at 1500 XP

      🔥 Combo System

      • Get 3 consecutive scores of 6+ to activate combo
      • Combo mode doubles all XP earned
      • Combo breaks on scores below 6

      🏆 Boss Battles

      • Each boss has a health bar
      • Good answers (6+) decrease boss health
      • Defeat the boss by reducing health to 0

      🎤 Voice Input

      Click the microphone button to answer questions using your voice, just like in a real interview!
